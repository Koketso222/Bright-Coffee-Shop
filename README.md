Bright Coffee Shop Sales Analysis

Project Overview - 
The Bright Coffee Shop Sales Analysis project is an end-to-end data analytics and business intelligence project developed to support the new CEO's objective of increasing revenue. The project uses transactional sales data to understand how Bright Coffee Shop is performing across products, product categories, stores, days and times. The project follows the complete analytics process, starting with the raw sales data, followed by data cleaning and transformation, creation of a final analysis table, development of multiple dashboards, and finally the presentation of business insights and recommendations to management. The main purpose of the project is not only to report historical sales, but to use the data to identify revenue drivers, performance gaps and potential opportunities for growth.

Business Objective - 
The central business question for this project is:
How can Bright Coffee Shop use its sales data to identify opportunities to increase revenue?
To answer this question, the analysis examines sales performance across different dimensions, including products, stores, categories, days and time periods. This allows management to understand what is contributing to revenue and where further investigation or action may be required.

The analysis focuses on:
•	Revenue and sales trends
•	Top-performing products and categories
•	Store performance
•	Best-performing days and times
•	Lower-performing areas
•	Potential revenue growth opportunities

Data Preparation - 
The project begins with the original raw Bright Coffee Shop sales dataset. The data contains transaction-level information such as transaction date and time, quantity sold, store, product, product category and unit price. The data was cleaned and transformed using Databricks SQL to create an analysis-ready final table. Additional fields were created to support revenue and time-based analysis, including month, day of week, day classification, hour, time bucket and revenue-related measures.

The project repository therefore contains the three key stages of the dataset:
•	Raw Data – the original sales data
•	Cleaned Data – prepared and standardised data
•	Final Table – the analysis-ready dataset used for dashboards and reporting

Analytics & Dashboards - 
To demonstrate the analysis across different business intelligence and visualisation platforms, the project includes dashboards developed using Excel, Power BI, Data Studio/Looker Studio, Databricks and Lovable. Each platform provides a different way of exploring and communicating the Bright Coffee sales data. The dashboards focus on revenue performance, product and category performance, store performance, and sales patterns across different days and times. The Lovable dashboard was specifically designed as an executive-facing Revenue Intelligence Dashboard. It brings the analysis together to help the CEO understand what is driving revenue and where potential opportunities may exist.

Key Analysis - 
The analysis looks at the business from several perspectives. Revenue analysis provides an overview of overall sales performance, while product and category analysis helps identify the products contributing most to revenue. Store analysis compares performance across locations, while time-based analysis examines when sales are strongest and when performance is lower. These insights can help management investigate opportunities such as targeted promotions, product strategies and improvements in lower-performing areas.

Key areas analysed include:
•	Total revenue and sales trends
•	Revenue by store and location
•	Revenue by product category and product
•	Units sold and transaction volumes
•	Average transaction value
•	Revenue by day and time
•	High- and low-performing products, stores and periods

Project Planning - 
The project was planned using Miro, which was used to structure the workflow from the sales transaction data through the ETL process, analysis and final presentation.
The planning process connected the different stages of the project:
Sales Transactions → Databricks ETL → Raw/Cleaned Data → Analysis Dashboards → CEO Presentation
This helped establish a structured approach to moving from the original dataset to meaningful business insights.

Final Presentation - 
The findings from the analysis were consolidated into a PowerPoint presentation for the CEO. The presentation focuses on the key findings from the sales data, including revenue performance, product and store performance, sales patterns, areas requiring attention and potential revenue opportunities.
The presentation demonstrates how the analytical findings can be translated into a clear business story for a senior management audience.
